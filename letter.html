My love,

I want you to know that I hear you. I know this isn’t something you can simply switch off, and I don’t want to make you feel like you should be able to. I can understand why the possibility of things getting worse scares you.

I hope you don’t start seeing yourself as someone who is broken or difficult because you’re struggling. You’re a person who is going through something painful, and you deserve understanding, patience and proper support.

You don’t have to justify your feelings, make them sound reasonable or pretend you’re doing better than you are. You deserve to be cared for even on the days when you don’t know how to care for yourself.

I hope we can find ways for you to feel safer, not just when I’m around, but even when you’re by yourself. And I hope you learn to treat yourself with the kindness you deserve.

Always in your corner, ♥

Hard nights end. The dawn always finds its way back to you.

Be gentle with yourself. Resting is not giving up.
